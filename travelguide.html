Travel Guide

1. Getting to Brunei

By Air

Visitors can travel to Brunei by flying into Brunei International Airport, which connects the country to various international destinations.

By Land

Visitors can enter Brunei by road through land checkpoints connecting Brunei and Malaysia.

By Sea

Visitors can explore available sea connections when planning their journey to Brunei.

2. Transportation

Public Buses

Use public buses to travel around selected areas of Brunei at an affordable cost.

Water Taxis

Experience traditional water taxis while travelling around Kampong Ayer and nearby waterways.

Car Rentals

Rent a car to explore different districts and visit attractions at your own pace.

Taxis & Ride-Hailing

Choose taxis or available ride-hailing services for convenient transportation around Brunei.

3. Accommodation

Luxury Hotels

Enjoy comfortable stays, excellent facilities and beautiful views at luxury hotels in Brunei.

Eco-Lodges

Stay close to nature and enjoy a peaceful experience surrounded by Brunei's rainforests.

Budget Hotels

Find affordable accommodation with basic facilities for a comfortable stay.

4. Travel Tips

Weather & Climate

Brunei has a tropical climate, so expect warm temperatures and occasional rainfall throughout the year.

Currency

The official currency is the Brunei Dollar (BND). Prepare some cash for shopping and daily expenses.

Local Etiquette

Respect local customs, dress modestly when visiting religious places and be polite to the local community.

Travel Essentials

Bring suitable clothing, sunscreen, an umbrella, personal items and important travel documents.
