Food & Culture

1. Traditional Bruneian Food

Ambuyat

Try Brunei's famous traditional dish made from sago starch, usually enjoyed with dipping sauce.

Nasi Katok

Enjoy a popular local meal consisting of rice, fried chicken and spicy sambal.

Kelupis

Discover a traditional snack made from glutinous rice wrapped in leaves.

Kuih Cincin

Taste a traditional sweet snack known for its unique ring-shaped appearance.

Kuih Penyaram

Try a traditional sweet cake with a crispy edge and soft centre.

Hati Buyah

Discover a traditional Bruneian dish prepared using seasoned beef or lamb lungs.

Pulut Panggang

Enjoy grilled glutinous rice wrapped in banana leaves, often filled with savoury ingredients.

Wajid

Taste a sweet traditional dessert made from glutinous rice, coconut milk and sugar.

Discover the festive atmosphere, family gatherings, traditional clothing and open-house celebrations.

Ramadan Celebrations

Experience the special atmosphere of Ramadan and explore local food bazaars before breaking the fast.
