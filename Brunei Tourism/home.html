About Brunei

Brunei is a small country in Southeast Asia. It is known for its rich Malay
culture, Islamic heritage, beautiful mosques, and lush rainforests. Despite its
small size, Brunei offers visitors a variety of cultural and natural attractions.

Where is Brunei Located?

Brunei is located on the northern coast of the island of Borneo
in Southeast Asia. It shares its land borders with the Malaysian state
of Sarawak. The capital city of Brunei is Bandar Seri Begawan.
